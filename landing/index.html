Petty

A tiny companion that lives on your Mac.

An animated desktop character that reacts while you work, stays out of the way, and brings a small bit of life to the desktop.

Petty, the productivity gym creature

Prototype features

Draggable desktop character

Move the companion anywhere and restore its last position on launch.

Idle, active, bored emotes

Readable lightweight states make the character feel responsive without invasive monitoring.

User-controlled visibility

Show, hide, reset position, and toggle always-on-top from the menu bar.

Collectible packs later

Future premium packs should be original characters with distinct personalities.

Character concept

Petty is a small original productivity creature that bounces during active work, relaxes while idle, and gets sleepy when the user has been away. The visual language should stay charming, readable, and legally clean.

Roadmap

  1. Prove native floating window behavior.
  2. Add original animation-ready character art.
  3. Add simple settings and visibility modes.
  4. Build a character gallery and pack model.

Privacy first

Petty should react to coarse activity signals only. It should not record keystrokes, capture screens, inspect other apps, or send telemetry.
